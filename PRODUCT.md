## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: Ukrainian- and Russian-speaking travellers from post-Soviet countries (Ukraine,
and the diaspora in the EU; also Kazakhstan, Belarus, Moldova) planning a first or repeat
trip to Rome. They are typically:

- researching 2 weeks to 6 months before arrival, on mobile, often at night;
- travelling as a couple, a family with children, or a small group of friends (2–6 people);
- unwilling to take a coach tour with 40 strangers, and unwilling to take an
  English-language tour they will only half understand;
- deciding between "several guides who all look the same" and needing one reason to write.

Secondary: repeat visitors who have already "done" the Colosseum and want depth
(authored routes, Caravaggio, underground Rome, Etruscans, the Roman countryside).

Third: English-speaking visitors — served, but not the audience the site is written for.

The job: *choose a person to spend a day of my expensive holiday with, and write to her
today.* The decision is about trust in a human being, not about a product SKU.

## Product Purpose

A personal site for Oleksandra Lukan (Лукань Олександра), a licensed guide and historian
in Rome. It exists to turn a stranger's research session into a booking enquiry by
WhatsApp/Viber or email, and to be the reference library her clients keep using during the
trip itself (museums, opening hours, holidays, Roma Pass, tax free, embassies, transfers).

Success = an enquiry message that already names a date, a tour and a contact address.

## Positioning

Not a tour operator and not a marketplace listing. A professional historian with
university training who lives in Rome and narrates it in the visitor's mother tongue —
including authored routes that exist nowhere else ("Рим скрізь століття", the Etruscans at
Villa Giulia, St Clement's underground Rome, Subiaco). The mechanism a neighbouring guide
cannot truthfully copy: *a historian's own research, delivered in Ukrainian or Russian,
one-to-one.*

- Booking channel: phone/Viber/WhatsApp `+39 38 99 66 44 55`, email
  `alessandralukan@gmail.com`. The enquiry must state date, chosen tour, time and a
  reply address.

  Museums, Colosseum by moonlight) — a real scheduling constraint, not a marketing line.
- Seasonal facts affect the offer: Colosseum night visits 23 Apr–9 Oct; night Vatican
  24 Apr–30 Oct; Rome empties in mid-August; sales (saldi) in January and July.
- Tours run in and around Rome (Lazio), in the Vatican, and in Vienna/Austria.

## Product Principles

1. **The person is the product.** Every surface must make one specific historian
   credible; interchangeable "professional guide" language is failure.
2. **Depth is the differentiator.** Publish the real, strange specifics (Pope Clement
   murdered in Crimea; the priest of Diana who had to kill his predecessor; Michelangelo's
   secret autograph at Farnesina). They are the proof.
3. **Her language, her words.** Content is translated, never flattened. UA is the primary
   voice; RU and EN are full peers in coverage.
4. **Enquiry over funnel.** One contact path, always within reach, always pre-filled with
   what she needs to answer (date, tour, time, reply address).
5. **Useful after booking.** The reference sections are part of the service, not SEO
   filler.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience skews older than an average tech product and reads long-form text on mobile:
generous body sizes, real contrast, no motion required to understand anything, tap targets
sized for a phone held one-handed. Cyrillic typography must be first-class, not a fallback.
